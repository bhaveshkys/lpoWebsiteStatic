🔷 Lexievo LPO – Services Overview
We provide comprehensive legal support services that empower attorneys and law firms to focus on high-value work while we manage the essential, time-consuming legal tasks. Our services are designed to improve efficiency, reduce costs, and maintain the highest standards of accuracy and confidentiality.

⚖️ 1. Estate Planning Support
Secure your clients’ futures with precise legal documentation.
We assist attorneys by preparing a full range of estate planning documents, including wills, trusts, powers of attorney, healthcare directives, and more. Our team ensures all documents meet jurisdictional requirements and are delivered promptly and error-free.
✅ Ideal for estate planning firms, elder law attorneys, and solo practitioners.
✅ Saves time and reduces internal drafting workload.

⚖️ 2. Probate Assistance
Simplify estate administration and reduce delays.
Our probate support team manages the preparation and filing of court documents, assembles asset and liability inventories, and assists with communication templates for beneficiaries and creditors. We help streamline the probate process, ensuring accuracy and timeliness for your clients.
✅ Supports probate attorneys, paralegals, and estate law firms.
✅ Reduces administrative pressure and boosts client satisfaction.

⚖️ 3. Bankruptcy Filing Support
Stay compliant and organized in high-pressure bankruptcy cases.
We offer accurate and efficient document preparation for both Chapter 7 and Chapter 13 filings. Services include gathering and organizing financial documents, drafting petitions, schedules, means test calculations, and creditor matrix preparation.
✅ Perfect for bankruptcy attorneys and financial legal services.
✅ Reduces errors and enhances workflow efficiency.

⚖️ 4. Civil Litigation Support
Power your litigation strategy with strong backend support.
From legal research to drafting complaints, answers, discovery documents, motions, and trial preparation, we provide reliable support for all stages of civil litigation. We work closely with legal teams to ensure timelines are met and documents are court-ready.
✅ Serves litigation attorneys, solo litigators, and boutique firms.
✅ Enables faster, more effective case preparation.

⚖️ 5. Immigration Case Support
Keep pace with evolving immigration laws and case volumes.
We provide comprehensive immigration support services including drafting visa petitions (H-1B, L-1, O-1, etc.), preparing USCIS forms, supporting asylum and green card applications, and managing RFE responses. We help your firm maintain consistency, compliance, and client confidence.
✅ Supports immigration law firms and high-volume practitioners.
✅ Helps maintain accuracy across complex documentation requirements.

⚖️ 6. Personal Injury Support
Focus on your clients while we manage the legal prep.
We assist in drafting demand letters, organizing medical records, preparing settlement documentation, and managing litigation documents related to personal injury claims. Our team ensures accuracy and compliance so your firm can focus on advocacy.
✅ Designed for PI lawyers and firms with heavy caseloads.
✅ Enhances productivity without expanding overhead.

⚖️ Bonus: Custom Legal Support (On-Demand)
If your legal needs go beyond our listed services—whether it’s contract drafting, legal research, compliance reviews, or document formatting—Lexievo LPO offers flexible, customized support tailored to your firm’s exact workflow.
